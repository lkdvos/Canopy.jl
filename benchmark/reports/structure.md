Timings are **not** in this table; it is pure structure and is therefore
machine-independent. `:fz2_u1_flat` is `fℤ₂ ⊠ U1Irrep` with the sector count
pinned at 4, so it isolates the growing axis inside the same symmetry group as
`:fz2_u1`. It is in `BENCH_SPACES` too, so its `SUITE["message"]["hex_vertex",
:fz2_u1_flat, χ]` timings can be read directly against these rows.

Two observations that hold across every row and matter for the blocked kernel:

1. `close_nblk == nsectors` exactly — the closing performs one gemm per
   *coupled* sector of `V_k`, so 7-13 gemms per closing on the `:fz2_u1`
   fixtures, not one per uncoupled tuple (`ntrees` is 73-253 there).
2. `nblocksec` is 2 for every graded fixture and 1 for `:trivial`, because the
   on-site tensor is `P ← V₁⊗…⊗V_N` and its coupled sector is fixed by the
   *physical* leg. It is therefore **not** a count of the kernel's gemms;
   `close_nblk` is.
